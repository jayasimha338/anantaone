Privacy & data use

AnantaOne collects the information entered in booking and provider-registration forms to arrange services, verify providers, assign jobs and manage payment records. Customer details are shared with the assigned provider only after a match. Provider PAN and verification details are held in the private team Google Drive and are not shown publicly or sent in booking emails.

Access to the Google Sheet must be limited to authorised AnantaOne team members. You may request correction or deletion of your information by contacting the team.
